## Added 2026-08-17 — second chat handover

Nine renders were handed over; **four were byte-identical to pieces already
here** (`foreground/tree-brace-a.png`, `midground/retaining-wall-a.png`,
`midground/timber-shoring-wall-a.png`, `midground/root-tunnel-a.png`) and were
not copied a second time — a piece that exists twice drifts twice. The other
five are new, all with real alpha:

- `background/forest-clearing-a.png` — see the Background section above; this
  handover is what filled that entry.
- `midground/fallen-log-a.png` — hollow fallen log with a mossy top run and a
  root-plate stump at the far end. The open mouth is big enough to read as a
  passage, which makes it the natural cousin of `root-tunnel-a`.
- `midground/timber-shoring-wall-b.png` — second take on the shored dirt wall:
  three timber posts, diagonal braces, corrugated strata, roots crossing the
  face, felt moss along the crest.
- `midground/log-bank-vignette-a.png` (1536×1024) — log-and-bank vignette,
  ferns and toadstools on the crest, a corrugated pipe mouth buried in the
  strata. Wider and taller than the flat camera wants: crop it rather than
  scaling it down, or the strata detail turns to noise.
- `props/stump-clearing-a.png` — cut stump with felled log rounds, a striped
  barricade and two marker flags. The one piece in the set that says *work
  happened here*, which is the whole point of World 3 being a **clearing and
  dig** rather than a forest.
