# EERI world art libraries

Optional source-art troves for Art, Design and Level agents. These are **not production assets** and are deliberately outside `eeri/assets/` and `assets/manifest.json`.

- **World 1 — Groundworks:** `world-1-library/`
- **World 2 — Pipeworks:** `world-2-library/`
- **World 3 — Forest clearing and digs:** `world-3-library/`
- **World 4 — Evening site under lights:** `world-4-library/`

## Agent rule

When the owner says **“check the World N library”**, inspect that world's `README.md` and `CATALOG.md` first. Reuse, crop, recolour or compose pieces when useful, but promote nothing into `eeri/assets/**` automatically.

The shared look remains the current Eeri art direction: Crafted World is the default answer, Tropical Freeze is seasoning; visibly hand-built materials, flat-plane-friendly environment pieces, and no accidental new gameplay affordances.
