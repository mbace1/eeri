# Eeri world identity pack

A world should be recognisable from a **cropped gameplay screenshot**, not only from a title card. This pack defines material, silhouette, environmental motion, accent props and sound behaviour for all four locked worlds.

The constant across all four is the 80/20 reference rule: handcrafted toy-diorama first, cinematic depth second. Identity never comes from colour grading alone.

## World reads

### 1 — Groundworks
Earth, timber, concrete forms, survey stakes and machine-yellow accents. Motion is dusty and pendular: hanging tape, loose signs, tiny distant digging. Shapes are horizontal stacks, trenches and mounds.

### 2 — Pipes & Water
Painted pipe sections, damp card, valves, grates, shallow water and patched concrete. Motion is cyclic: drips, steam, valve ticks and ripples. Shapes are circles, elbows and vertical pipe runs.

### 3 — Forest Clearing & Digs
Cut timber, bark-card trunks, root mats, brush bundles and temporary site fencing. Motion is organic but still crafted: branch bob, paper leaves, log roll, rope sway. Shapes are tall trunks broken by excavated cuts.

### 4 — Evening Site Under Lights
The same physical craft world after workday light falls: floodlights, reflective tape, portable lamps, silhouettes and lit cabins. Motion is sparse and luminous: rotating beacon, moth-like paper flecks, distant crane silhouette. Darkness never hides gameplay geometry.

`worlds.js` makes these rules queryable by agents and future runtime dressing systems.
