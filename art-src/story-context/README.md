# Eeri light story / context pack

Eeri does not need dialogue scenes. The story is the worksite changing because the player passed through it.

## Narrative structure

- **Title:** the existing one-line premise remains enough: Eeri adventures in a world of work machines and robots.
- **World entry:** one short line plus one physical establishing image/object. Shown once on first entry; replays can skip straight to the level.
- **Levels:** no story text. The level's job and changing environment carry the context.
- **World exit:** the clock-out/building payoff is the answer to the world-entry setup.

## Four world questions

1. **Groundworks:** the site begins as earth, trenches and temporary forms. The visible question is: *what are they building here?*
2. **Pipes & Water:** the site now needs water routes and crossings. The world becomes more connected as pipes are placed and water is managed.
3. **Forest Clearing & Digs:** construction reaches the wooded edge; timber, roots and excavation share the set.
4. **Evening Site Under Lights:** the same larger worksite is now recognisable as something built over the whole adventure. Work lights make the final stretch feel like closing time, never a timer.

No named NPCs, lore logs, villains or cutscene exposition are added by this pack.
